Why Wilpattu

Ancient lakes. Dense forest. Wild silence.

Wilpattu National Park is Sri Lanka's largest and one of its oldest national parks, covering a vast wilderness of forest, open grasslands and natural lakes known as villus. Those changing landscapes create the feeling that every game drive is its own story.

From leopard country to elephants, sloth bears, buffalo, spotted deer, jackals, crocodiles and rich birdlife, Wilpattu rewards patience, quiet observation and a good guide.
